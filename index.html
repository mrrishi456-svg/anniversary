Happyyyyyyyy 4th Anniversaryyyyyyy, my loveeeeeeeee! 😫😘🫂

Today is not just another day for me. Today marks four beautiful years of us, four years of loving each other, fighting for each other, missing each other, and choosing each other despite everything life has put us through.

Roshni, you know what? Our journey was never easy. We faced so many family issues, so many struggles, so many difficult situations that sometimes even staying together felt impossible. There were moments when everything seemed to be falling apart. We even stopped talking for months, and those months were honestly some of the hardest times for me. I missed you, I missed your voice, your presence, your love, and everything about you. But somewhere deep in my heart, I always knew that our story wasn't over.

And look at us todayyyyy! After all those tears, struggles, misunderstandings, distance, and difficult days, WE MADE IT! 🫂

With the grace and blessings of our Mahadev Ji, we are celebrating our fourth anniversary together. I genuinely believe that if two hearts are meant to be together, no matter how difficult the journey becomes, they will always find their way back to each other. And that's exactly what happened with us. 🧿

My love, I don't just want you for today, tomorrow, or a few more years. I WANT YOU FOR THE REST OF MY LIFE. I want to grow old with you, celebrate every little happiness with you, fight through every difficult situation with you, hold your hand when life gets hard, and love you even more with every passing year.

If there are a thousand universes, I want you in every single one of them. If there are a thousand Janams, I want to fall in love with you in every single Janam. I don't want a different love story or a different person. I WANT YOU, ONLY YOU, EVERY TIME, IN EVERY UNIVERSE, IN EVERY JANAM. 👩‍❤️‍💋‍👨

You have become my heart, my happiness, my peace, my strength, my everything. You are not just Roshni to me; you are literally the Roshni of my life. And one day, I want to change that beautiful name from Roshni Solanki to Roshni Rishi Chouhan. 😫😘🌹 I want that day to be one of the most beautiful days of our lives.

I promise you that I'll keep choosing you, loving you, respecting you, and standing beside you. I know we aren't perfect, and our relationship isn't a fairytale without problems, but I don't need a perfect relationship. I just need YOU, and I want us to keep making our imperfect love story more beautiful together.

Thank you for not giving up on us. Thank you for being my person. Thank you for coming back into my life and giving our love another chance.

And lastlyyyyy, meri jaan, I just want to sayyyyy...

I LOVEEEEEEEEEEEE YOUUUUUUUUUUU SOOOOOOOOOOOOO MUCHHHHHHHHHHH, MY DARLINGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGG! 😫😘🫀

I LOVEEEEEEEE YOUUUUUUUUU FROM THE BOTTOMMMMM OF MY HEARTTTTTTTTT. No words, no paragraphs, and no amount of love can ever explain how much you mean to me.

Happy 4th Anniversary, my forever girl. Here's to us, our love, our future, and all the beautiful years waiting for us.

YOU ARE MY TODAY, MY TOMORROW, MY EVERY JANAM, AND MY FOREVER. 🫀🫂🌹👩‍❤️‍💋‍👨😘
